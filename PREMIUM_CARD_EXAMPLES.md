## Why This Works

**Glance Test (1 second):**
1. Hero image tells you what it is (campus, restaurant, hotel, etc.)
2. Star rating tells you if it's good (4.8 = excellent)
3. Stat tells you why it's special (100% Pass Rate = confidence)
4. CTA tells you what to do next (View Profile)

**Second Glance (3 seconds):**
- Highlights tell you what makes it unique
- Tagline plants an emotion
- Verify badge builds trust

**Third Glance (5 seconds):**
- You're ready to click "View Profile"
- No description needed
- Confidence is built

**Conversion Path:**
1. See image → stop scrolling
2. See rating → trust building starts
3. See stat → "Oh wow, 100% pass rate!"
4. See highlights → "IEB, Boarding, Grade R–12 — perfect"
5. See tagline → "I like their philosophy"
6. See CTA → click "View Profile"
