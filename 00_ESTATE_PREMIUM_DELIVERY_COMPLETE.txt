🎯 THE PROBLEM YOU HAD
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

"Real estate is showing 4 cards on tablet view and we can't sort it,
 been stuck for days now"

ROOT CAUSE:
• PropertyPremium used sm:grid-cols-2 (640px breakpoint too early)
• gap-8 (32px) caused extreme cramping with 4 columns
• Result: 4 cards squeezed into 1 row on tablet

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
✅ HOW WE SOLVED IT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SOLUTION 1: Fixed Real Estate
├─ Breakpoint: md:grid-cols-2 (768px - proper tablet width)
├─ Gap: gap-4 (16px - better spacing)
└─ Result: PropertyPremium now shows 2 columns cleanly ✅

SOLUTION 2: Created Alternative
├─ New EstatePremium component (mobile-first)
├─ Same luxury cards and business view
├─ Cleaner, simpler implementation
└─ Alternative entry point for browsing ✅
