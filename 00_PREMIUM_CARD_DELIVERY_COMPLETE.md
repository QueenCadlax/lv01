## 🏗️ The 10-Element Card Structure

Every card displays (in priority order):

1. **Hero Image** (320px tall) — What stops scrolling
2. **Verify Badge** — "✓ Verified" or "🏆 Featured" 
3. **Institution Type** — "PRIVATE SCHOOL", "FINE DINING", etc.
4. **Name** — Large, bold, serif
5. **Location** — 📍 With icon
6. **Rating** — ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.8 (58) — Trust metric
7. **Three Chips** — Top 3 highlights only
8. **Tagline** — One line, italic, memorable
9. **Premium Stat** — Gold box confidence driver
10. **CTA Button** — "View Profile →"

This drives confidence in <5 seconds:

✅ NEW: **"100% Pass Rate"**

Why? Answers "Why choose this?" immediately.

Examples:
- Education: "100% Pass Rate", "34+ Years", "11 Scholarships"
- Property: "R8.5M", "5-Bed Estate", "1,250m²"
- Restaurant: "Michelin Star", "200+ Wines"
- Hotel: "99% Satisfaction", "Forbes Top 50"
